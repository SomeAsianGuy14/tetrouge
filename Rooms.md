# Encounter rooms
- Wishing Well: PLayers offer one coin at a time and there is a chance they get some reward (40% technique, 20% keystone, 40% consumable). The chance for a reward starts at 1% but increases by 1 after every unsuccessful offering. Upon success the probability resets to 1%. They can leave whenever they want
- Shop: The encounter room is just a shop
- Altar (Technique): Players offer one of their techniques and receive a random technique or leave
- Altar (Keystone): Players offer one of their keystones and receive a random keystone or leave
- Library: Players can choose a technique from a random list of 10 or leave
- Robbers: Players can choose to either lose all of their gold or enter combat against an enemy
- Unfortunate Head Trauma: Player loses a random technique
- Pickpocket: Player loses 50% of their gold (Show the numeric value)
- Museum: Player can choose to gain a randomly determined keystone or leave
