# Damage types

- Burn: 1 Garbage line every 2 seconds into the buffer
- Poison: 1 Garbage line every 3 seconds directly onto the board
- True: Permanent unclearable line at the bottom of the board

# Enemies

- New class of elite enemies designated by their own special combat tile, One guaranteed spawn per floor
- Bonus effects applied on top of base attack

- Corrupted Mage: Randomly fills unfilled cells in certain patterns
- Possesed Blade: Deletes a random 3 row section on the board
- Crimson Drake: Taking unblocked damage burns you for 4 seconds
- Venomous Archer: Taking unblocked damage poisons you for 6 seconds

# Bosses

- The Tide: Every 30 seconds deal 1 true damage
- The Serpent: Applies permanent poison for the duration of the fight
- *Modified* The Furnace: Applies permanent burn for the duration of the fight

# Keystones

- *Modified* Burning Board: renamed to poisoned blood: Become permanently poisoned, All attacks deal x2
- *Modified* Glass Cannon moved to keystone: Take 10 lines of true damage, All attacks deal x2 
- Blazing Heart: Become permanently burned, All attacks deal x2
