# Encounters

- *New* Tutor: Players can choose to gain +1 level in a mastery of their choice
- *New* Sleeping Beast: Players can choose to participate in a combat or leave. the combat is 1 tier higher than the current diffuculty of combats in the room, winning grants them a random technique and coins
- *New* Laboratory: Contains 3 random consumables that the player can take
- *New* Demonic Deal: Only availble if player has over 3 levels of a mastery. Players can trade 3 levels of their mastery for 150 coins
- *New* Mimic: Appears like a treasure chest room but if players try to claim the treasure it triggers a combat with a mimic
- *New* Beggar: Players can offer 50 gold or leave. If they offer the gold they gain a random rare or lower technique
- *New* Map room: Player can choose to examine the map or leave. Examining the map reveals all the fogged rooms.
- *Modified* Museum reworked into treasure chest
- *Modified* Pickpocket encounter will have a corresponding combat generated later in the path where players fight the pickpocket to reclaim their gold
- *Modified* If the player has a technique or keystone relating to speed they avoid the falling rock and do not forget a technique
