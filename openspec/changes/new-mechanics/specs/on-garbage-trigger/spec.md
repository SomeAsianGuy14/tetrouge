## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Garbage-received technique trigger
RunManager SHALL emit a callback when garbage packets are generated by the enemy timer. Techniques with garbage-reactive effect types SHALL be evaluated in this callback.

#### Scenario: Garbage triggers technique evaluation
- **WHEN** the enemy timer fires and generates garbage packets
- **THEN** all techniques with garbage-reactive effect types SHALL be evaluated

### Requirement: Thrash technique
Thrash SHALL be a common technique that deals 1 damage per garbage line received directly to the enemy whenever the player receives garbage.

#### Scenario: 3 garbage lines received
- **WHEN** the enemy sends 3 garbage lines and the player has Thrash
- **THEN** 3 damage SHALL be applied directly to the enemy quota

#### Scenario: Shield absorbs garbage
- **WHEN** shield absorbs 2 of 3 incoming garbage lines
- **THEN** Thrash SHALL deal 3 damage (based on lines generated, not lines that hit the board)

### Requirement: Retribution technique
Retribution SHALL be a common technique that buffs the player's next attack by +3 damage whenever garbage is received.

#### Scenario: Garbage received buffs next attack
- **WHEN** the enemy sends garbage and the player has Retribution
- **THEN** the player's next line clear SHALL deal +3 additional damage

#### Scenario: Multiple garbage events before clearing
- **WHEN** the enemy sends garbage twice before the player clears
- **THEN** the next clear SHALL deal +3 (not +6 — the buff does not stack)
