## Techniques

- *Moved from Keystone* Sharpen -> Every 6th piece is Honed
- *NEW* Barricade: Every 6th piece is reinforced 
- *NEW* The Best Defense: 25% of your attack is converted to shield
- *NEW* Last Stand: The First time each round your board height exceeds 80% gain 10 shield
- *NEW* Preperation: After a Quad your next piece is Honed
- *NEW* Backpedaling: After your combo count exceeds 5 your next piece is reinforced
- *NEW* Golden Blade: If your clear contains a gold piece it deals an additional 2 damage

## Keystones

- Simple Shield -> Start each round with 5 shield
- Legionnares Shield -> Start each round with 10 shield
- *NEW* Extraordinary Bag: Every 7 pieces one of your pieces is enhanced with a random bonus
- *NEW* Charging Up: Every 10th piece is amplified
- Midas Touch -> Every 7th piece is gilded
- *NEW* Jack of All Trades: All piece enhancements are triggered a second time
- *NEW* Refined: Honed pieces deal an additional 2 damage
- *NEW* Armored: Reinforced pieces grant an additional 2 shield
- *NEW* Overclocked: Amplified pieces are 50% more effective
- *NEW* Polished: Gilded pieces grant an additional coin

## Consumables

- Lottery Ticket: Your next 3 pieces are enhanced randomly
- Sharpening stone: Your next 4 pieces are Honed
- Charged Battery: Your next 2 pieces are Amplified
- Gold Leaf: Your next 4 pieces are gilded
- Steel Plates: Your next 4 pieces are Reinforced
